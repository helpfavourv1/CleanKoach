Free up phone storage.

Find and delete the photos, videos and audio you no longer need. Everything runs on your device.

Large files

Lists your biggest photos, videos and audio, sorted by size, so the files that really fill your storage come first. Select what you no longer need and see the total space before you delete anything. Nothing is deleted until you choose it.

Duplicates

Finds photos, videos and audio that are exact copies by comparing their contents, not just their names. Every group shows how much space you can reclaim, keeps one copy and lets you choose which one stays.

Similar photos

Groups near-identical shots, such as burst photos and repeated takes, by how they look. The largest file in each group is suggested as the one to keep, you can pick a different one, and the rest are ready to delete with the reclaimable space shown.

Screenshots

Gathers every screenshot on your phone into one list, so you can clear them out in a single pass instead of hunting through your gallery.

Photo optimizer

Compresses the photos you select and saves each smaller copy to a CleanKoach folder, leaving the original in place. It only lists photos big enough to be worth compressing and shows how much space you saved as it works.

Video optimizer

Compresses the videos you select with three presets: high quality, balanced or small file. Each result is saved as a new, smaller video in a CleanKoach folder, with progress and the space saved shown as it runs.

Activity Monitor

Shows which apps you opened, how many times, and how long each one ran in the foreground over the last 24 hours. It uses Android's optional Usage Access permission, and the data stays on your device.

Wi-Fi Security

Checks your current connection: whether you are online, the type of encryption and authentication, whether a test site's security certificate is valid to catch SSL stripping and interception, and whether a proxy is set. Run it again any time.

Network Speed

Measures your download speed with a live reading while a test file downloads from Cloudflare's public speed-test server. It measures download only, not upload or ping.
