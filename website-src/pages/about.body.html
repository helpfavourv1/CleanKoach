About CleanKoach

CleanKoach is a storage cleanup app for Android, built and maintained by Ak, an independent developer. It helps you get space back without risking the photos and videos you care about.

What it does

  • Finds what takes space: large files, identical duplicates, similar photos and screenshots.
  • Compresses what you keep: Photo optimizer and Video optimizer show the space saved.
  • Clears the leftovers: Clean Up removes trashed photos and videos plus app cache in one tap.
  • Watches your phone: Activity Monitor, Wi-Fi Security and Network Speed.

How it works

Your photos, videos and audio are analysed on your phone. Your media, usage data and scan results are never uploaded, and the app has no accounts. You choose what to remove, and Android asks you to confirm before media is deleted. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How it is funded

CleanKoach is free to use. Ads appear on the home screen and between actions. Pro removes them.

Languages

The app is available in 44 languages. Choose yours in Settings, or browse the full list.
