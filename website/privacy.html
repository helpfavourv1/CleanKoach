Summary

CleanKoach is a storage cleanup app for Android. Its purpose is to find and delete media you no longer need. Everything it does runs on your device. There is no CleanKoach server, no user account, and no telemetry. The only outside party involved in normal use is Google AdMob, which serves the ad banner on the home screen.

What we never collect

CleanKoach does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of the following with any party:

  • Your photos, videos, or audio files
  • The results of any scan
  • Your usage statistics — which apps you opened, when, or for how long
  • Your Wi-Fi network name, MAC address, or encryption type
  • The results of any Wi-Fi security test or speed test
  • Your device model, Android version, or any device identifier beyond the advertising ID that Google Play services exposes
  • Your name, email, phone number, or any account information. CleanKoach has no accounts.

What stays on your device

Media files

With your permission, the app reads your photos, videos, and audio files to find large files, byte-for-byte duplicates, near-identical photos, and screenshots. Files are hashed, grouped, and scored on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Computed hashes

To speed up repeat scans, the app caches SHA-256 and perceptual hashes of your media in a local database. These hashes never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Usage statistics

If you grant Usage Access, the app reads your app launch history over the last 24 hours to power Activity Monitor. This data is read in-memory each time you open that screen and is not persisted to disk or uploaded.

Wi-Fi information

The Wi-Fi Security feature reads the encryption type of your current connection and inspects the certificate chain of a fixed HTTPS endpoint. No payload is transmitted beyond the TLS handshake. No result is uploaded.

Settings and preferences

Theme, language, notification preferences, and consent state are stored on your device using Android's DataStore. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

CleanKoach shows a banner ad on the home screen, served by Google AdMob. When an ad is served, Google may collect your advertising ID and standard ad-interaction data as described in Google's privacy policy. This data is not linked to any account, because the app has no accounts.

If you purchase Pro, the banner is removed and the app stops requesting ads. Google Play Billing handles the purchase; Google receives the transaction data as the payment processor.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is served. If you decline, no ads are served. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings → Privacy options.

Google's advertising privacy disclosures: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Permissions explained

  • Photos, videos, audio — required to scan and clean your media library. Without it, the media tools are unavailable.
  • Usage Access — powers Activity Monitor only. Optional. You can use every other feature without it.
  • Notifications — sends weekly cleanup reminders and storage alerts, only if you enable them.
  • Internet — loads the AdMob banner and runs the download speed test.
  • Network state — reads whether you have internet access and what type of Wi-Fi you are on.
  • Wi-Fi state — reads the current Wi-Fi encryption type for the Wi-Fi Security screen.
  • Billing — communicates with Google Play for the optional Pro purchase.
  • Boot completed — re-schedules the weekly cleanup reminder after a device restart, only if you enabled it.

Children

CleanKoach is not directed at children under 18 and is not listed in Google Play's Families program. It does not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, uninstalling it removes all local state.

Your rights

Because CleanKoach does not collect or transmit personal data from your device, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf.

If you want to reset the app's local state, uninstalling the app removes everything. If you want to opt out of interest-based advertising, you can do so in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or by purchasing Pro.

Under the GDPR and similar regulations, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Since the app operates entirely on-device, there is no controller-side data to contest.
