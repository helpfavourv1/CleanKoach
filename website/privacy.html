CleanKoach

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

CleanKoach is a storage cleanup app for Android. This policy explains what data the app accesses, what it does with it, and what it never does.

Data we never collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers or on anyone else's servers:

  • Your photos, videos, or audio files
  • The results of any scan
  • Your usage statistics (which apps you opened, when, or for how long)
  • Your Wi-Fi network name, encryption type, or security test results
  • Any account information. CleanKoach does not have accounts.

Data processed on your device

The following data is read and processed entirely on your device. It never leaves your phone through this app.

Photos, videos and audio

With your permission, the app reads your media library to find large files, duplicates, similar photos, and screenshots. Files are analyzed on-device. Nothing is uploaded.

Usage statistics

If you grant Usage Access, the app reads your app launch history over the last 24 hours to power the Activity Monitor. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded or shared.

Wi-Fi information

The Wi-Fi Security feature reads the encryption type of your current connection and inspects the certificate chain of an HTTPS test endpoint. No payload is transmitted beyond the TLS handshake, and no results are uploaded.

Settings

Your preferences — theme, language, notification settings — are stored on your device only.

Advertising

CleanKoach shows ads on the home screen through Google AdMob. When ads are served, Google may collect your advertising ID and standard ad-interaction data as described in Google's privacy policy. This data is not linked to any account, because the app has no accounts.

If you upgrade to Pro, ads are removed and no advertising ID is collected by the app from that point forward.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you will be asked for consent before any ads are served, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Privacy options.

Permissions

  • Photo, video and audio access — scans and cleans your media library.
  • Usage Access — powers Activity Monitor only. Optional.
  • Notifications — sends weekly cleanup reminders and storage alerts, if you enable them.
  • Internet — loads ads and runs the network speed test.
  • Network state — reads whether you have internet access and what type of Wi-Fi you are on.

Children

CleanKoach is not directed at children under 18. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Because CleanKoach does not collect or transmit personal data from your device, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. If you want to reset the app's local state, uninstalling the app removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
