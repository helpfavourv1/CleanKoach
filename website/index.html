What it does

1

Large files

Lists your biggest photos, videos and audio, sorted by size, with quick filters for anything over 10 MB.

2

Duplicates

Finds files that are byte-for-byte identical, keeps one copy, and lets you delete the rest in a single step.

3

Similar photos

Groups near-identical shots — the burst frames, the almost-the-same-pose — and marks the best one as the one to keep.

4

Screenshots

Lists every screenshot on the device so you can clear them out in one pass. Sorted newest first.

5

Photo optimizer

Re-encodes photos as WebP at quality 85 — typically 25–35% smaller than the original JPEG, with no visible difference.

6

Video optimizer

Re-encodes videos at High, Balanced, or Small presets. Original files are never touched — you get new copies and choose what to keep.

7

Activity Monitor

Shows launches, last-launch time, and foreground time per app for the last 24 hours. Requires Usage Access; optional.

8

Wi-Fi Security

Inspects your connection's encryption type and certificate chain, and tells you whether SSL stripping or interception is present.

9

Network Speed

Measures your download speed against a fixed, app-owned endpoint. Download-only by design.

Privacy, stated plainly

Your photos, videos, audio, usage statistics, and network information stay on your device. CleanKoach never uploads them. There are no accounts. There is no telemetry.

The only outside contact is Google AdMob for the banner on the home screen. Pro removes the banner. In the EEA and UK, ads are only served after you consent.

Built for Android, not around it

CleanKoach is a native Android app written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material 3. It talks directly to the Android media framework, usage statistics, Wi-Fi stack, and Media3 transcoder — the same components the system itself uses. No cross-platform layer, no shortcuts, no web views doing work that should be native.

Free, with an optional upgrade

Every feature is free. The app shows a small ad banner on the home screen to keep the lights on. A one-time Pro purchase removes it. That's the entire business model — no subscriptions, no feature gating, no pay-to-clean.

Who it's for

If your phone is at 90% storage and the Photos app won't tell you what to delete, CleanKoach is for you. It's built for the everyday case, not the power-user case: open the app, tap CLEAN UP, see what it found, decide what goes. Simple enough to use in thirty seconds, careful enough that nothing gets deleted without your say-so.
