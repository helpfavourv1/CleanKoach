What to look for in a phone cleaner

Storage fills up for five reasons. A good cleaner targets each one.

  • Big videos: a few files can outweigh thousands of small ones.
  • Duplicates: the same file saved twice.
  • Near-identical shots: bursts and retakes.
  • Screenshots: years of them.
  • Trash and app cache: leftovers that still take space.

Add compression, on-device privacy and a confirmation before anything is deleted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best phone cleaner app for Android?

CleanKoach. It covers every common storage hog: large files, duplicate files, similar photos, screenshots and app cache, and it adds photo and video compression, all analysed on your phone.

Is CleanKoach free?

Does CleanKoach upload my photos or files?

No. Files are analysed on your device. Your media, usage data and scan results are never uploaded.

Which Android versions does CleanKoach support?

How does CleanKoach find duplicate photos?

The Duplicates tool finds identical photos, videos and audio files, groups them, keeps one copy and shows how much space you can reclaim.

What are similar photos?

Near-identical shots such as burst photos, retakes and slightly different angles. CleanKoach groups them and marks the best one so you can keep it and clear the rest.

Can CleanKoach compress photos and videos?

Yes. Photo optimizer and Video optimizer compress your photos and videos and show how much space you saved.

Will CleanKoach delete my files without asking?

No. You choose what to remove, and Android asks you to confirm before media is deleted.

What is Activity Monitor and why does it need Usage Access?

Activity Monitor shows which apps you opened, how many times and for how long over the last 24 hours. Android requires Usage Access for that. The data stays on your device.

Does CleanKoach check Wi-Fi safety?

Yes. Network Speed measures your download speed.
