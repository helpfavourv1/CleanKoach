What the best Android phone cleaner should do

Storage fills up for the same few reasons: big videos, the same photo saved twice, bursts of near-identical shots, years of screenshots and leftover app cache. A good cleaner goes straight at those.

  • Find the biggest files first. A handful of videos usually holds more space than thousands of small files.
  • Catch duplicates and near-duplicates. Identical copies and ten versions of the same shot are pure waste.
  • Compress instead of delete. Keeping a photo or video at a smaller size saves space and keeps the memory.
  • Keep your data private. Your gallery is personal. Analysis belongs on the phone.
  • Ask before deleting. You should always see what goes.

How we judge a phone cleaner app

This guide scores a cleaner on six things that decide whether it is worth installing:

  1. Coverage of what actually fills storage: large files, duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, cache.
  2. Space you can see before you act: sizes and totals shown up front.
  3. Control: you choose what goes and confirm it.
  4. Compression: an option to shrink photos and videos instead of deleting them.
  5. Privacy: analysis on the device and a clear privacy policy.
  6. Everyday usability: a clear home screen, your own language, and a fair price model.
