How to stop a phone cleaner popping up

Updated: 2026-10-12 · By Ak

Quick answer

To stop a phone cleaner popping up, find out what is showing it, then switch that off. The cause is usually a website with notifications allowed, an app with permission to show over other apps, or a cleaner app's own alerts. Long-press the pop-up to see which app sent it, then block or uninstall it.

A pop-up that says your phone needs cleaning is not always from a cleaner you installed. Work through the sources below, from most to least likely.

Where the pop-up comes from

  • A website: you allowed notifications from a site in your browser, and it now sends alerts.
  • An app's notifications: an installed app sends alerts that look like system warnings.
  • An app showing over others: an app with permission to display over other apps.
  • A page inside your browser: a fake alert on a web page, which closes when you close the tab.

Find out which app or site it is

  1. Long-press the notification. Android shows the app or site name.
  2. Check where it appears. In the notification shade it is a notification. Over another app it is an overlay. Inside a web page it is the site.
  3. Note the name, then use the table below.

Are phone cleaner apps safe?

Block website notifications in Chrome

Open Chrome, then Settings, then Site settings, then Notifications. Move any site you do not trust to the blocked list, or turn notifications off entirely. Chrome can also use quieter messaging, so sites can ask without a pop-up.

Check with Google Play Protect

Open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, tap Play Protect and run a scan. Make sure Scan apps with Play Protect is on. It checks apps for harmful behaviour and can warn you about or remove them.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a phone cleaner keep popping up?

Usually a website you allowed notifications from, an app with permission to show over other apps, or a cleaner app sending alerts.

How do I find which app is showing the pop-up?

Long-press the notification. Android shows the app or site that sent it.

How do I stop website notifications on Android?

In Chrome, open Settings, Site settings, Notifications and block the site. Other browsers have a similar setting.

What is Display over other apps?

An Android permission that lets an app draw on top of other apps. Turn it off for any app that does not need it.

Is a pop-up saying my phone has a virus real?

Almost never when it comes from a web page or an unknown app. Close the tab and do not tap it. Run a Play Protect scan if you are unsure.

Does CleanKoach pop up on my screen?

No. CleanKoach does not display over other apps. Its weekly reminder and storage alerts are optional and off until you turn them on.

How do I turn off CleanKoach reminders?

Open CleanKoach, go to Settings and switch off the notification options, or use Android's notification settings for the app.

What if the pop-up returns after I uninstall the app?

Another app or a site you still allow is sending it. Repeat the steps and run a Play Protect scan.

Should I install another cleaner to stop pop-ups?

No. Remove the source instead. Another app adds another thing that can show alerts.
