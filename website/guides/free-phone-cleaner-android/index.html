What is free in CleanKoach

All of it. Pro does not unlock tools, because there are none locked.

  • Large files: your biggest photos, videos and audio, sorted by size.
  • Duplicates: identical files grouped, one copy kept.
  • Similar photos: near-identical shots grouped, the largest suggested to keep.
  • Screenshots: every screenshot in one list.
  • Photo optimizer and Video optimizer: compress what you keep.
  • Clean Up: clears trashed photos and videos and the app's own cache in one tap.
  • Activity Monitor, Wi-Fi Security and Network Speed: app usage, a connection check and a download speed test.

How the ads work

CleanKoach shows ads through Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of the home screen, the settings screen and the tool screens, and a full-screen ad now and then after you finish an action such as a clean-up.

Ads never appear while a scan is running, on the Pro purchase screen or on top of a permission or consent dialog. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you are asked for consent before any ads are served, and you can change your choice later in the app's Privacy options.

What Pro does

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. It removes all ads. It does not unlock features, because every feature is already free. If you reinstall CleanKoach or change phones, tap Restore purchase. The price on the Pro screen is the one Google Play charges in your country.

How to judge any free cleaner

Free apps need to earn money somehow. Before you install one, check:

  • Are the tools really usable? Some apps scan for free, then lock the clean-up behind a paywall.
  • Does it say how it earns? Ads and a one-time purchase are easy to explain. Vague claims are not.
  • Does it ask for sensible permissions? Media access is expected. Contacts, messages or location are not.
  • Does it need an account? A cleaner has no reason to.
  • Does it frighten you? Fake virus alerts and countdown timers are warning signs.

Is CleanKoach really free?

Yes. Every feature is free to use. The app shows ads, and a one-time Pro purchase removes them.

Is there a trial or a time limit?

No. There is no trial that expires and no feature that stops working. Pro only removes ads.

What does Pro cost?

The price is shown on the Pro screen inside the app. It is set through Google Play for your country, and you pay once.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase.

Where do the ads appear?

As a small banner at the bottom of the home, settings and tool screens, and as an occasional full-screen ad after you finish an action such as a clean-up.

Do I need an account to use CleanKoach?

No. CleanKoach has no accounts.

No. Files are analysed on your device. Your media, usage data and scan results are never uploaded.

Yes. Install CleanKoach, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Google account that bought it.
