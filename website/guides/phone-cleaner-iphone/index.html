Phone cleaner for iPhone

Updated: 2026-10-12 · By Ak

Quick answer

CleanKoach is an Android app and there is no iPhone version today. To free up space on an iPhone, use the tools built into iOS: check Settings, General, iPhone Storage, follow its recommendations, offload apps you rarely use and turn on Optimize iPhone Storage if you use iCloud Photos.

CleanKoach is for Android

CleanKoach is a native Android app, built for Android 9 and newer, and it is available on Google Play. There is no version for iPhone or iPad today, so there is nothing to install on an iPhone.

Check what is using your iPhone storage

  1. Open Settings, then General.
  2. Tap iPhone Storage. You see recommendations and a list of apps with the space each uses.
  3. Tap an app's name for details.

Apple notes that cached and temporary data might not count as usage in this list.

Use the built-in recommendations

If storage is full, tap Show All in the Storage section to see recommendations. Tap Enable to turn one on, or tap it to review what you can delete. On iOS 17 and later, the Recommended for You list in Apple's photo storage steps can flag photos, large files or backups that you might not need.

Offload or delete apps

Open an app's entry in iPhone Storage and choose to offload it. That frees the space the app takes up and keeps its documents and data. Deleting the app removes its related data as well.

Manage photos and videos

Photos and videos are usually the biggest item. If you use iCloud Photos, turn on Optimize iPhone Storage: full-resolution originals stay in iCloud and smaller copies stay on the phone when space is needed. Apple also suggests merging duplicate photos and videos. This depends on having enough iCloud storage.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a CleanKoach app for iPhone?

No. CleanKoach is an Android app and there is no iPhone version today.

How do I clean up my iPhone storage?

Open Settings, General, iPhone Storage, follow the recommendations, and offload or delete apps you do not use.

What does offloading an app do?

It frees the space the app uses and keeps its documents and data. You can reinstall the app later.

How do I find what is using my iPhone storage?

Settings, General, iPhone Storage shows a list of apps and what each uses, plus recommendations.

How can I save space on iPhone photos?

With iCloud Photos on, turn on Optimize iPhone Storage so smaller copies stay on the phone. You can also merge duplicates in Photos.

Does iPhone have a duplicate finder?

Apple suggests merging duplicate photos and videos in Photos to free up space.

Can I use CleanKoach on an iPad?

No. CleanKoach is for Android phones and tablets.

Which Android versions does CleanKoach support?

Android 9 and newer.

Where can I get CleanKoach?

On Google Play, for Android.
