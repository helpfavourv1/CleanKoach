Phone cleaning kit

Updated: 2026-10-12 · By Ak

Quick answer

A good phone cleaning kit is small: a soft microfibre cloth, a soft dry brush, safe disinfecting wipes and a charging-port-safe way to remove lint. For the files inside the phone, add CleanKoach, a free app that clears storage.

Kits sold online are often padded with items you will not use. This list covers what Apple and Google actually describe, with the digital half covered by CleanKoach.

The physical kit

  • Soft, lint-free cloth: Apple and Google both name it first. A microfibre cloth is the usual choice.
  • Soft, dry brush: for dust at the edges and the speaker grille.
  • Disinfecting wipes: 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes, which Apple and Google allow, with no bleach.
  • A torch: to look into the charging port before cleaning it.

The digital half

A cloth cannot free storage. CleanKoach is the digital kit: Large files, Duplicates, Similar photos, Screenshots, the Photo and Video optimizers and one-tap Clean Up. Every feature is free.

What to leave out

  • Aerosol sprays and bleach: Apple advises against them.
  • Compressed air: Apple and Google advise against it.
  • Metal picks: they can damage ports and speaker mesh.
  • Anything abrasive: it can scratch the screen and wear its coating.

A simple routine

  1. Weekly: wipe the screen and back with a dry cloth.
  2. Monthly: check the charging port and speaker grille, and review Large files in CleanKoach.
  3. Now and then: disinfect with a wipe and remove the case to clean under it.

Frequently asked questions

What should a phone cleaning kit include?

A soft lint-free cloth, a soft dry brush, 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes and a torch. Add CleanKoach for storage on Android.

Do I need a special phone cleaning kit?

No. The core items are cheap and common. Both Apple and Google advise a soft, lint-free cloth.

Is isopropyl alcohol safe for phones?

Apple and Google both allow 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes on the outside. Do not use bleach.

What should I never use?

Aerosol sprays, bleach, abrasive materials, compressed air and metal tools in openings.

Does CleanKoach clean the hardware?

No. CleanKoach clears storage. It does not clean screens, speakers or ports.

Can CleanKoach replace a cleaning kit?

No. A kit cleans the outside. CleanKoach cleans the files inside. You need both.

Is CleanKoach free?

Yes. Every feature is free. A one-time Pro purchase removes the ads.

Is there an iPhone version of CleanKoach?

No. CleanKoach is for Android only.

How often should I clean my phone?

Wipe the screen weekly, check ports and speakers monthly, and review storage monthly.
