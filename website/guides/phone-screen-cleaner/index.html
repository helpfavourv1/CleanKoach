Phone screen cleaner

Updated: 2026-10-12 · By Ak

Quick answer

No app cleans a phone screen, and CleanKoach does not claim to. To clean the glass, turn the phone off, unplug it, wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth, use a slightly damp one for grime, never spray liquid directly on the phone and keep moisture out of the openings.

A smeared screen is the easiest phone problem to fix, and the easiest to make worse. Both Apple and Google give the same core advice.

Why an app cannot do this

A screen collects oil and dust on the glass. CleanKoach works on files and storage, so it has no effect on the glass. Use it for storage and a cloth for the screen.

The basic clean

  1. Turn the phone off and unplug all cables.
  2. Wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth. A dry microfibre cloth removes dust, smudges and streaks.
  3. For grime, use a slightly damp cloth, or a screen wipe or glasses cleaner on Pixel.
  4. Wipe gently. Avoid excessive rubbing.
  5. Let it dry before you turn it on.

Disinfecting the screen

Apple says you may use a 70% isopropyl alcohol wipe, a 75% ethyl alcohol wipe or Clorox Disinfecting Wipes on the outside of an iPhone, then wipe with a soft, slightly damp, lint-free cloth. Google says household disinfecting wipes or 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes can be used on a Pixel, including on the screen. Apple advises against bleach and hydrogen peroxide, and Google advises against bleach.

Wipes, not liquid: do not pour or spray anything onto the phone.

What to avoid

  • Aerosol sprays and bleach: Apple says not to use aerosol sprays, bleaches or abrasives.
  • Spraying directly: do not spray cleaner onto the phone.
  • Moisture in openings: keep it out of the ports and speakers.
  • Abrasive materials: Apple says cleaning products and abrasives can diminish the oil-repellent coating and might scratch the screen.
  • Soaking: do not submerge the phone in any cleaning agent.

Screen protectors and cases

If you use a screen protector, clean the protector the same way. Remove the case now and then and wipe the phone and case separately so grit does not scratch the back or edges.

Frequently asked questions

Can an app clean my phone screen?

No. A screen is glass. CleanKoach clears storage and cannot clean a screen.

What is the safest way to clean a phone screen?

Turn the phone off, unplug it and wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth. Use a slightly damp cloth for grime.

Can I use isopropyl alcohol on my phone screen?

Apple allows a 70% isopropyl alcohol wipe on an iPhone's outside and Google allows 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes on a Pixel, including the screen. Use a wipe, not liquid.

Can I use bleach or hydrogen peroxide?

No. Apple advises against both on iPhone, and Google advises against bleach on Pixel.

Should I spray cleaner on the screen?

No. Do not spray cleaner directly on the phone. Use a wipe or a damp cloth.

Why does my screen get greasy?

Skin oil and dust collect on the glass. A dry microfibre cloth removes most of it.

Can cleaning damage the screen coating?

Apple says cleaning products and abrasive materials can diminish the oil-repellent coating and might scratch the screen, so keep it gentle.

How often should I clean my phone?

As often as it looks dirty. Wipe the screen when you notice smudges.

Does CleanKoach clean my screen?

No. CleanKoach clears storage, not glass.
