1. What the app does

CleanKoach finds large files, duplicate media, similar photos, and screenshots on your device, and can compress photos and videos. All processing happens on your device. The app does not upload your media to any server.

2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the content you choose to delete. The tools that list files (Large files, Duplicates, Similar photos and Screenshots) show you what is selected before you delete it. CLEAN UP on the home screen empties the items already in Android's trash and clears CleanKoach's own cache.

On Android 11 and newer, Android asks you to confirm deleting photos, videos and audio. If you switch on the optional Media management permission for CleanKoach in Android Settings, deletions you start in the app go through without Android asking each time. You can switch it off at any time.

Back up anything you cannot afford to lose before using bulk-delete features. CleanKoach cannot recover items once they are deleted.
